# Research questions

Status: proposed questions, October 19, 2026.

1. **Discrimination:** How does performance on blinded EQ difference tasks vary between listeners and with the EQ pair, track, equipment and session?
2. **Preference consistency:** How repeatable are preferences within and between sessions? How do ties and reported uncertainty relate to repeatability?
3. **Relationship:** Does independently measured discrimination predict preference repeatability? Can listeners discriminate reliably while having weak or context-dependent preferences?
4. **Subgroups:** Do preference patterns differ across prospectively defined discrimination and consistency groups after accounting for listening context and comparison coverage?
5. **Weighting:** What question would consistency weighting answer, and how would that population differ from an all-listener analysis?
6. **Skill scoring:** Can task-specific discrimination performance support an Elo-like score with calibrated uncertainty? Is a multidimensional profile more appropriate than one overall listener score?

Preference disagreement with a majority is not evidence of error. No universal correct EQ preference is assumed.
