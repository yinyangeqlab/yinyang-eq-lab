## Captured research goals

Investigate whether listeners differ in their ability to perceive differences between EQ targets, and whether those differences help explain population preference patterns. Measure how reliably listeners repeat preferences under comparable conditions. Explore whether consistency should inform an analysis, rather than assuming all response patterns should be weighted equally. Compare independently defined listener subgroups. Investigate a separate Elo-like measure of listening discrimination or task performance.

## Interpretation and limits

Discrimination, preference consistency and agreement with other listeners are separate quantities. A listener can reliably prefer a minority choice. The claim that inconsistency implies inability to hear a difference is a hypothesis to test, not an established conclusion.

The lossless-versus-lossy analogy motivates a discrimination experiment; it does not establish equivalent perceptual mechanisms or results. The experimental and analysis suggestions in the linked notes are proposed synthesis, not additional recovered conversation content.

## Fit with the existing project

The research protocol describes participant-balanced target preference scoring and leaves identical-target diagnostics out of preference weighting. Existing design notes caution against treating dissenting tastes as inaccurate. Listener skill scoring is distinct from target preference scoring and does not reinstate historical target Elo.

This proposal does not authorize changing target rankings, listener weights, eligibility rules or historical records. See [research questions](research-questions.md), [analysis plan](analysis-plan.md) and [decisions](decisions.md).
