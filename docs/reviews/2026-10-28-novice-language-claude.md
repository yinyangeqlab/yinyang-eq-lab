## 1. The ten most serious barriers

1. **The first click lands on a research-ethics form, not a listen.** "Find my target" opens the data-choices dialog (`components/comparison-data-settings.tsx:32-66`), which has a nickname field, a research appeal, two preset buttons, five numbered sections, historical studies and file-contribution rights. The one decision a novice must make (agree to keep A/B picks) is phrased as "Comparison sharing is required to enter the listening tools" (`:37`), and the Agree button stays disabled until a yes/no about "Live activity monitoring" is chosen (`:46-48`, `:66`). On the Listen page agreeing then reloads the whole page (`app/shootout/page.tsx:620`).

2. **A "headphone measurement" is mandatory and never explained.** Every listening path blocks on "Select a headphone measurement before starting a test." (`app/shootout/page.tsx:312`) or "Pick your headphones in step 1 to start." (`lib/personal-start-status.ts:26`). The picker speaks lab language: "Search model or measurement source", "Public measurement index · creator sources", "Rig: 5128", "Rig not listed" (`components/headphone-picker.tsx:105-117`). The empty state's primary button is "Upload a measurement" (`app/shootout/page.tsx:606`), which opens a form asking for "Measuring device / rig · 711, KEMAR, 5128" and "Nozzle diameter, mm" (`components/measurement-manager.tsx:125,130`). A novice does not know that typing their headphone model name is the intended path.

3. **"Target" is the site's core noun and is never defined.** Welcome says "Hear two headphone EQ targets side by side", "Find my target", "TARGET A … TARGET B" (`app/page.tsx:60-65`); the tab title says "Compare headphone target curves" (`app/layout.tsx:13`). To a newcomer a target is a goal, not a sound profile. "Curve", "frequency response CSV", "preference curve" and "target curve" compound this on every page.

4. **Six listening products compete on the first screen.** Welcome presents "CHOOSE YOUR TOURNAMENT" with Personal Tournament, Blind Research and Open Comparison, then "Please contribute to both." (`app/page.tsx:76`); the mode tabs add "Blind EQ comparison · Upload two presets · Private" (`components/tournament-mode-tabs.tsx:8`); Rankings and Discover add Community Preferences reference checks; the shareable shootout is a seventh flow. The sidebar's "Listen" item goes to `/shootout?tab=shootout` (`components/alpha-shell.tsx:15`), which is Blind Research, while Welcome's main button goes to Personal. The mode descriptions use "non-elimination pool", "benched", "Combined Research Rankings", "identical-filter checks remain diagnostic".

5. **The five-second matched-passage lock is explained in research terms.** Voting is locked until each side has 5 s and the same passage overlaps 5 s, but the overlap rule only surfaces as an error: "Replay the same passage under A and B until at least 5 seconds overlap." (`app/shootout/page.tsx:438`). The strip shows "Voting locked · Hear 5 s of the same passage on A and B to vote" with a toggle "Require 5-second comparison ON" and a help text about "consented participant comparisons", "free-listening records" and "ranking eligibility" (`components/matched-passage-control.tsx:15-41`). The easy way out, "Play both targets", explains itself only in a tooltip (`components/music-panel.tsx:945`).

6. **Loudness and level matching are presented as LUFS settings.** "−16 LUFS · Locked", "Normalization off", "Matched source loudness, as in Open Comparison", "−14.3 LUFS measured · matching to −16.0 LUFS" (`app/shootout/page.tsx:606`, `app/shootout/personal/page.tsx:307,334`, `components/music-panel.tsx:269`). Welcome's fairness list says "Blind Research uses a −16 LUFS source target … Perceived loudness is not matched between targets." (`app/page.tsx:69`), which is honest but reads as "the test is unfair" to someone who cannot parse it.

7. **Results and rankings lead with statistics a novice cannot act on.** My results opens with "Total trials / Recorded votes / Private trials / Repeated pair agreement", "accuracy SD: 0.707" and "Identical-target check reliability: … (Beta(2, 2) prior)" (`components/results-profile.tsx:52-53`); the blind-session summary shows "same-filter accuracy 50% (SD 0.707)" (`app/shootout/page.tsx:606`); Rankings shows "SCORE ± MODEL SD", "participant groups" and "fractional Bradley–Terry scoring model starts at 1500 with a 350 SD prior" (`:607`). The plain answer ("which sound did I like") sits below.

8. **Channel matching leaks into every mode.** The safety dialog shown to everyone says "Disable system/device EQ, sound enhancements and device channel matching. The website handles supported channel matching" (`components/before-you-listen.tsx:15`); Open shows "Channel balance · Auto Match / My PEQdB preset" (`app/shootout/page.tsx:606`); Personal shows "Channel matching · Auto / Uploaded / Off" and "Auto needs Left and Right responses for channel correction." (`app/shootout/personal/page.tsx:308`). None of this matters to a first listen.

9. **Tuning's export step presents fifteen engineering options with no recommendation.** "Finalize preference curve · Generate 10, 15, and 20 band EQs with PEQdB unsmoothed, plus the original Standard and Precise options", then cards with "Fit error 0.84 dB · Preamp −3.5 dB", filter rows like "PK · 105 Hz · +2.30 dB · Q 0.70", and download buttons "TXT / Equalizer APO / EasyEffects / Qudelix 5K" with no "which one is for my device" guidance (`app/preference-tuning/page.tsx:659-669`).

10. **The same thing has three names depending on the screen.** Sidebar "Listen · Tune · Music library" vs page headers "Tournament · Listening Pool · Preference Tuning" (`components/alpha-shell.tsx:14-22` vs `app/preference-tuning/page.tsx:629`, `app/music-library/page.tsx:176`); "Use Your Music" vs "Bring Your Own" vs "Own music blind" (`components/research-music-source.tsx:14-15`, `components/results-profile.tsx:60`); tie buttons "Unsure / Tie", "No Preference / Tie", "Can't tell" (`app/shootout/page.tsx:606`, `app/shootout/personal/page.tsx:355`, `components/embed-shootout.tsx:330`); run / session / trial / round / comparison / check used interchangeably; "My results", "My data", "My space", "My profile" as four separate places; a maintenance page still titled "YinYang EQ League" (`lib/maintenance-options.ts:68`).

## 3. Recommended beginner journey and progressive disclosure

Default path (Welcome primary button, and the sidebar's Listen item, should both lead here):

1. **One sentence, one button.** "We play your music two ways. You pick the one you like. Names stay hidden." Button: "Start listening". Secondary link: "Other ways to listen" (research mode, named comparison, EQ-file comparison, shareable test).
2. **Headphones.** "Which headphones are you using?" with a model search as the only visible control (`components/headphone-picker.tsx` already supports this via "Browse database"). Two small fallbacks: "Not in the list? Try the quick test without headphone adjustment" linking to the shareable shootout, which applies no correction; "I have a measurement file" opening the expert form. Explain in one line why this matters: "We use a lab measurement of your model to adjust the sound."
3. **Agree in one screen.** Two buttons: "Keep my A/B picks only" and "Also help with optional research", each with a two-line consequence. Nickname, monitoring, music tags, historical studies and file rights live behind "More choices". Default monitoring to No instead of forcing a radio. Keep the privacy notice link. Do not reload the page after saving.
4. **Safety.** Keep Before You Listen, trimmed to volume safety and "turn off any equalizer or sound enhancement". Drop channel matching from this dialog.
5. **Listen.** Show only: Sound A, Sound B, "Play both (same 5 seconds on each)", a single progress line ("A 3.1 s · B 0.0 s · play the same part on both"), and the three choice buttons with one tie label. Song picker shows "Site songs (random) / My own song". Everything else (level matching, left/right balance, passage shuffle, dynamics, audio stats, after-EQ levels, song rating, file fingerprint, shortcut scope) sits under one "Advanced" expander.
6. **After each pick.** "Saved: you preferred B." Reveal names. One link "Where this goes" opens the data-destination text that currently fills the status line.
7. **Results.** "Your favorites so far on {headphones}" with the podium's plain note ("Provisional; check your top 3 on different music"). "Fine-tune my favorite" leads to Tuning with thump / voices / sparkle sliders. Statistics, checkpoints, consistency checks and research filters under "Details".
8. **Save it.** In Tuning, one "Make my EQ" button, one recommended file, a "Which file do I need?" list by device or app, and "Show all 15 options" for experts. Keep the disclosure that downloads are recorded for private research.

Disclosure tiers to apply site-wide: Tier 0 always visible (what to do now); Tier 1 "Details" (why, where data goes); Tier 2 "Advanced" (LUFS, channel balance, passage requirement switch, dynamics, stats, EQ variants); Tier 3 "Research notes" (ITU citations, Bradley–Terry, Wilson intervals, privacy ledger). Scientific caveats stay, but a plain sentence should always precede them.

## 4. Terminology glossary

| Current label | What it means to a newcomer | Suggested plain label |
|---|---|---|
| target, target curve, EQ target | a recipe for how headphones should sound, drawn as a line from bass to treble | sound profile (keep "target" in parentheses for experts) |
