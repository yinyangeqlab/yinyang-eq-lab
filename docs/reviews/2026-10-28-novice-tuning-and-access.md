# Beginner review: tuning, preset comparison and access

Reviewed October 28, 2026 against deployed update125, source `c9d6cc0e66804710f1948a71bbc568d75cfe7642` in `work/peqdb-release-final-20261028`. This is a source-based review of likely comprehension problems, not observed failures in a user study. No application edits or production activity occurred. Source references below are relative to that immutable release.

Priority: **High** blocks understanding, a necessary choice, recovery, or an informed decision; **Medium** adds avoidable effort; **Low** is polish or specialist-only material. Proposed wording is draft copy, not a statement that a new flow already exists.

## Good wording and patterns to retain

- “Shape the sound you want” is an understandable outcome. The four-step Tuning help, Undo/Redo, explicit reset consequences and clear “Choose music to begin” are useful foundations.
- The private comparison explains five seconds on each side, allows “No preference” and Skip, warns that the session disappears on refresh, states early-reveal consequences, and does not promise a successful file save merely because a download started.
- “This passage is silent or too quiet to compare. Choose another passage” and “Playback was interrupted. Press Play to try again” pair a problem with a recovery action.
- Linking instructions retain meaningful access/deletion warnings; simplification must make these easier to understand, not conceal them.

Long-page visibility findings need a permitted synthetic or hidden-test-account browser review before implementation. Legal wording was reviewed for readability only, not legal adequacy.
