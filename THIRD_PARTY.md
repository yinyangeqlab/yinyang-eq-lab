Each of the 32 included tracks’ individual [Bandcamp](https://virt.bandcamp.com/album/shantae-half-genie-hero-ost) pages states CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (checked October 23, 2026); “Title” and “Let’s Go Shopping” are all rights reserved and excluded. None of those recordings is included here or covered by Apache 2.0. `data/shantae-audio.json` maps each of the 32 track IDs to the local path (`/music/shantae/<id>.<ext>`) where authorized copies are expected; the audio files are not in this repository. `data/music-waveform-hashes.json` records SHA-256 hashes of the 32 Shantae files and of the 24 Jason J. Chevallier files below.
- `data/permission-music.json` lists 24 recordings by Jason J. Chevallier that the site uses with the artist’s permission. They are **all rights reserved**: no Creative Commons or other license to visitors is claimed, and the recordings are not included here or covered by Apache 2.0. The permission record is kept privately. See `docs/JASON_MUSIC_IMPORT_2026-10-23.md`.
- `data/music-waveforms.json` (118 entries) and `data/listening-pool-waveforms.json` (200 entries) hold cached waveform summaries: 512 bins of left/right minimum and maximum peak levels per recording (about 2 KB each). They are analysis data, not audio, and are outside the Apache grant. The first file covers the 32 Shantae tracks, 62 library recordings and the 24 permission-only recordings; the second covers 200 library recordings, including the 32 Shantae tracks. For those 200, the licenses recorded in this repository’s catalogs are CC BY 4.0 (118), CC0 (27), CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (34, including the 32 Shantae tracks), CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 (10), CC BY-SA 2.0/3.0 (9) and public-domain mark (2); sources: `data/music-candidates.json`, `data/listening-pool-local.json`, `data/listening-pool-license-evidence.json` and the Bandcamp pages above. Each recording keeps its own license and attribution.
- `data/autoeq-catalog.json` is a file-name and hash index of the [AutoEq repository](https://github.com/jaakkopasanen/AutoEq), which is MIT licensed; its notice is in `vendor/autoeq.LICENSE.md`. The index has no measurement points.
- `data/jaysaudio-catalog.json` is a file-name and hash index of [Jay’s Audio](https://github.com/JaysAudio/JaysAudio.github.io), which is 0BSD licensed. The index has no measurement points.
- Both indexes link to external measurements. Measurement creators retain their rights; raw measurements are not included.

No PEQdB target curves or preset exports are included. The separate LGPL AutoEQ engine described below is the only PEQdB code used by this integration. The working project's PEQdB and other outside-contributor curve seeds were excluded because this package does not establish redistribution rights for them.

The names-only [live target inventory](docs/TARGET_INVENTORY.md) credits the target families used or added on the separate live service, including Tryptify, AutoEq and MrChillStorm sources. No numerical target data is included, including the MIT-licensed experimental curves. Their inclusion on the live service does not relicense this repository or imply creator endorsement. New live imports require explicit redistribution terms; the earlier pool's licensing audit remains incomplete.
